# Allergy-Friendly Website

## CAS Service Project – Project Plan

### 1. Project Idea

I want to create a free website for people who have food allergies, intolerances, or dietary restrictions.

The main goal is to make everyday life easier for people who sometimes feel ignored or have difficulty finding suitable food.

The website will focus mainly on:

* Gluten / wheat-free food
* Milk / dairy-free food
* Egg-free food
* Peanut-free food
* Nut-free food

The website will especially focus on food, recipes, restaurants, cafés, products, and useful information in Debrecen.

---

### 2. Why I Am Creating This

People with food allergies or intolerances can have difficulties when:

* choosing what to eat
* cooking at home
* finding suitable recipes
* eating at restaurants
* finding suitable cafés
* buying packaged food
* understanding ingredients
* finding reliable information

I want to create one simple place where this information is easier to find.

---

### 12. Safety & Accuracy

This is extremely important.

The website must NOT claim that a restaurant or product is completely safe unless this has been verified.

Food allergies can be serious.

The website should explain that:

Information on this website is for general information only. People with severe allergies should always confirm ingredients and cross-contamination risks directly with the restaurant or manufacturer.

Restaurant information should be checked before publishing.

Product ingredients should be checked against current packaging whenever possible.

---

### 13. Things the Website SHOULD NOT Do

The website should NOT:

* Give medical diagnoses
* Tell someone to stop taking medication
* Replace a doctor or dietitian
* Guarantee that a restaurant is 100% allergy-safe
* Give dangerous medical advice
* Shame people for their food choices
* Promote extreme diets
* Make people afraid of food
* Use unreliable information as facts
* Copy recipes, photos, or text from other websites without permission

---

### 14. Privacy

If the website eventually allows users to create accounts or submit experiences:

* Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
* Do not publish someone’s private information.
* Allow users to remain anonymous when possible.
* Have clear rules for submitted content.
